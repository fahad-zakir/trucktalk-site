FAQ

What does TruckTalk do with load boards?

It reads the image, extracts stops, miles, deadhead, rate, and pay, speaks a summary, then answers follow-up questions over Bluetooth until you message a contact.

Is this WhatsApp or a full TMS?

No. TruckTalk is Telegram-first today — send, read, call, and Drive Mode announcements. Not a TMS replacement.

Will it send without me knowing?

No. Sends and calls require confirmation — voice yes/no or Confirm/Cancel on screen.

What languages work?

English, Spanish, Russian, and Turkish for hearing commands and speaking replies.
