Rigly privacy

Rigly reads Telegram for you and answers questions about load photos. This page says what the app handles.

Telegram

You sign in with your own Telegram account. Rigly stores that login on its server so it can read incoming messages and send the replies you confirm. Messages you ask it to read, including photos, are processed to speak them and to answer your questions.

Voice

The microphone and speech recognition hear your commands. Spoken replies are played on your phone or headset.

Load photos and AI

When you ask about a load image, Rigly sends that image and your question to an AI service to extract the load and answer. Those answers are spoken back to you. Do not send a photo you do not want used for that.

Notifications

With Drive Mode on, Rigly can send a notification when a new message arrives and the app is not on screen. Drive Mode also mutes Telegram’s own alerts while it is on.

What we don’t do

Rigly does not sell your messages, photos, or voice. The app is not for children.
