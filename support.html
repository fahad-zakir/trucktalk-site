Rigly support

Rigly hears your Telegram messages, reads load photos, and lets you ask follow-up questions by voice.

Sign in with the Telegram account you drive with. Turn Drive Mode on to mute Telegram’s own alerts while Rigly reads new messages. Say yes when it asks about a load photo, then ask things like the rate, the stops, or the miles.

Link Telegram

  1. Open Rigly and finish the short walkthrough if it appears.
  2. In the Link your Telegram panel, enter the phone number on your Telegram account (include country code, e.g. +1…).
  3. Tap Continue. Enter the code Telegram sends, then open Telegram and approve the login.
